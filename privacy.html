Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 29, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Neon Snake ("the App"). This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal sensitive user data (such as location, contacts, or photos).

We use a simple username and password system for saving high scores. This data is stored securely on our servers solely for the purpose of maintaining the leaderboard and game progress.

3. Children's Privacy

Our App is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond the username they choose to create.
